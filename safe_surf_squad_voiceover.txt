Slide 14:
This is called phishing.  
It means someone tricks you… into giving your username or password.  
They pretend it’s something good… but it’s actually a trap.

Slide 15–17:
There are different ways people can trick you.  
Smishing – fake messages or texts.  
Vishing – phone calls from strangers.  
QR phishing – fake QR codes.  
If something looks too good to be true… it probably is.

Slide 18:
So what should Adam do?  
Report it. Tell a parent or trusted adult. Contact the platform.  
It’s not your fault if you get tricked… but you must act fast.

Slide 19–20:
One month later…  
Adam makes another mistake.  
Someone says: “Give me your password and I’ll help you win.”  
Good idea or bad idea?  
Exactly… never share your password.

Slide 22–25:
Let’s talk about data.  
Data means information about you.  
Your name… your location… what you like… what you do online.  
This creates your digital footprint.  
Everything you do online leaves a trace.

Slide 26–31:
Never share:  
Your full name, address, phone number, school, or password.  
If you wouldn’t tell a stranger… don’t share it online.

Slide 32–36:
Passwords should be strong.  
Use letters, numbers, and symbols.  
Mix upper and lower case.  
Don’t reuse passwords.  
Your password is like your house key. Keep it safe.

Slide 37–39:
Let’s play a game.  
Which password is stronger? adam2015 or T!g3r$R0ck?

Slide 40–44:
To stay safe online:  
Think before you click.  
Use strong passwords.  
Update your devices.  
Keep accounts private.  
Adam learned this… and now he is safe.
